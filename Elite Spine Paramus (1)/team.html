Owner & Clinic Director

Michael Yarish, DPT, GTS, Cert. DN

As the Owner and Clinic Director of Elite Spine and Sports Care of Paramus, Dr. Michael Yarish, DPT, is dedicated to providing a welcoming, results-driven environment where patients can truly heal. For over seven years, Dr. Yarish has championed a comprehensive biopsychosocial approach to physical therapy. This means he takes the time to listen to your unique story, understanding that true recovery goes far beyond just treating an isolated symptom—it involves looking at how your lifestyle, environment, and personal goals all play a role in your health. After earning his undergraduate degree from The College of New Jersey in 2014 and his Doctorate in Physical Therapy from West Coast University in Los Angeles in 2017, Dr. Yarish returned to New Jersey to build a practice centered on cutting-edge, compassionate care.

To ensure his patients receive the highest quality of treatment, Dr. Yarish has pursued advanced specialist certifications that set Elite Spine and Sports Care apart. As a Graston Technique Specialist and a certified practitioner in both Dry Needling and the McKenzie Method, he possesses a highly specialized toolkit to tackle chronic pain, break down stubborn scar tissue, and resolve complex spinal and joint issues. Rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all routine, he designs highly customized rehabilitation programs tailored to your specific body and lifestyle, accelerating your recovery and helping you avoid future injuries.

Dr. Yarish understands the frustration of being sidelined by pain because he lives a highly active lifestyle himself. When he isn't in the clinic, you can usually find him working on his golf swing, following a dedicated power-building routine in the gym, or taking his motorcycle out for a ride. Because he knows exactly what it takes to perform at a high level, he brings that same drive and empathy to every patient's recovery. Alongside his wife, Annalisse, Dr. Yarish is proud to be an active part of the local community, and he looks forward to welcoming you into the clinic to help you get back to doing the things you love, completely pain-free.

Occupational Therapist

Jessica Jozefiak, OTR/L

Jess is an occupational therapist at Elite Spine and Sports Care of Paramus who is passionate about providing individualized, patient-centered care to help patients reduce pain, restore function, improve independence, and return to the activities that matter most to them. Born and raised in Pennsylvania, Jess graduated from Susquehanna University in 2018 with a BS in Psychology and the University of the Sciences in Philadelphia in 2021 with a MS in Occupational Therapy. She began her occupational therapy career in a skilled nursing facility in Delaware before moving to New Jersey and transitioning to outpatient rehabilitation. Drawing on her experience in both skilled nursing and outpatient rehabilitation, Jess takes a functional approach to occupational therapy, focusing on upper-extremity rehabilitation, hand therapy, and helping patients regain the skills necessary for everyday independence.

She values patient collaboration, striving to create a supportive environment where patients feel confident and empowered throughout their recovery. Her goal is to help each patient achieve meaningful improvements in function and return to their daily routines, work, and recreational activities with greater comfort and confidence.

Outside of work, Jess enjoys trying new restaurants, spending quality time with friends and family, and relaxing at the beach.

Physical Therapy Aide

Joseph Strobino

Physical Therapy Aide

Cole DeMagistris

Cole De Magistris brings a combination of athletic experience, leadership, and personality to Elite Spine and Sports Care. A graduate of Bates College, Cole continued his academic and athletic career at Sacred Heart University, pursuing his MBA while competing as an offensive lineman and Captain at the Division I level. With firsthand experience in the demands of competitive athletics, Cole understands the importance of strength, recovery, and injury prevention. He also keeps things fun around the clinic with “Cole’s Poll,” a would-you-rather-style question that sparks conversation and friendly debate among patients and staff. His athletic background and outgoing personality make him a great addition to the Elite Spine and Sports Care team!
